FAQs

Questions, answered clearly

Can all my loans be combined into one EMI?

Not always. It depends on the lender's products, your credit profile, income, outstanding balances and other eligibility rules.

Will my EMI definitely become lower?

No. A new loan may have a different tenure, interest rate and fees. Compare the total repayment cost as well as the EMI before deciding.

Does LoansBharti approve or disburse the loan?

LoansBharti provides sourcing assistance. The lending institution makes the final decision on approval, rate, terms and disbursal.

Is submitting an enquiry a guarantee of approval?

No. An enquiry is only a request for contact and initial review; it is not a loan application approval.
